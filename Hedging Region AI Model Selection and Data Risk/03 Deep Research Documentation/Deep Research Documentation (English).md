## Lessons and open items

**Lessons**

- Hand the research tool the facts we have already verified and ask it for new items only. Our long brief invited it to repeat old text.

- A request for a verification log does not produce one. We must check the output against the pages ourselves, and a short list of dated, opened pages is cheaper to check than a long report.

- Regulator and provider pages are best read directly. Where our tools could not open an official page, the search documents list it as found but not read, and name the secondary source used instead.

- A narrow prompt, such as three sources per search with the licence text copied, is the better design for any further Gemini run. Round 3 used that design and still marked every page opened, so each item needed our own check. Rejecting needs the same care as accepting: Gemini said FMTI had no licence, and only opening the repository page showed CC-BY-4.0.
